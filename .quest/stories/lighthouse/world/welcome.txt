Welcome, new keeper!

The lamp went out in the storm, and ships need it tonight. I've
gone to the mainland for parts. You can get it lit without me.

How this place works:
  - Places are folders. To go into one, type Set-Location and its
    name, like this:  Set-Location cottage
  - Notes are files. To read one, type Get-Content and its name.
  - To see what's around you, type Get-ChildItem.
  - To go back out of a folder, type:  Set-Location ..
    (two dots mean "the folder I'm inside of")

The spare matches are somewhere in the cottage. My diary will
help. The lamp is at the very top of the tower.

  - Mara

P.S. Long names? Type the first few letters and press Tab: the
     terminal finishes the name for you.
P.P.S. Screen getting full? Clear-Host wipes it clean.

NOTICE FROM THE ARCHIVIST

The Star Map, our oldest and most precious map, has gone
missing from the map room. I need a detective.

What we have:
  logs\       the door log for every night in October, one
              file a night. Hundreds of lines: don't read
              them all, search them.
  staff.txt   who carries which badge number.
  lockers\    every staff locker, somewhere in there.

Please:
  1. Search the logs for the map room. Something happened
     there at night.
  2. Find whose badge it was, and write their name into a
     new file, case-notes.txt.
  3. Count every line in the logs with their badge number,
     and add that number to case-notes.txt.
  4. Find their locker. I have a feeling about it.

Useful:
  Select-String "map-room" logs\*        every line with map-room
  ... | Measure-Object                   count what comes through
  Get-ChildItem lockers -Recurse -Filter "*x*"
                                         find names with x in them
  Get-Help Select-String                 help for any command

While you're here: the catalog lists one book twice, I lost
my gold bookmark in the stacks years ago, and somewhere in
the stacks is a letter that starts "Dear Archivist".

  - Dr. Imani Osei, Archivist
